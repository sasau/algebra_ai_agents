# Step 3 — the question, before any house rules

**What you do:** ask a plain question about the project and **keep the answer
verbatim**. This is a control, exactly like `00-baseline.md` was in the prompt
lab.

**Why it matters:** in the next step you will change the agent's behaviour
without touching this question. To see that, you need this answer written down
first — not remembered, written down. The whole finding is a diff, and you
cannot diff against an impression.

**What to watch:** the *shape* of the answer, more than its content. How long
is it? Does it cite file paths? What tone does it take? Does it lead with
architecture, or with how to run things? Those are the things `CLAUDE.md` will
move in step 04, and they are easy to miss unless you noted them deliberately.

> **Do NOT `/clear`** — not before this, and not after. The clear comes
> *inside* step 04, at a specific moment.
