# Step 0 — the baseline (deliberately bad)

No role. No material. No constraints. No format. Just the question, the way
most people ask it on a bad day.

**Do not improve this prompt.** It is the control you measure everything else
against. Paste it exactly, save the answer verbatim, then move on.

What you should expect back: a competent, well-organised paragraph about adding
indexes, avoiding `SELECT *`, and checking the query plan. It will be *true*.
It will also be advice that could have been written before you asked, because
none of it depends on your query. That is the trap — **a generic answer and a
specific answer are equally fluent.**
